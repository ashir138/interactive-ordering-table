The Problem

Dine-in ordering is stuck in 2005.

The table is the last un-digitised touchpoint in the restaurant — and it's costing both sides.

🙋

For diners

Static menus — you can't see the pizza you're building or its price & nutrition until it arrives.

Dead waiting time — 18–25 minutes of staring at a wall.

Order errors and awkward back-and-forth with busy staff.

the real cost 🍽️

For restaurants

Rising labour costs & chronic staff shortages.

Zero first-party data on what customers actually crave.

Slow table turnover and missed upsell at the moment of appetite.

“Waiting & guessing” is the worst part of eating out — for everyone at the table.

Why Now

Three tailwinds are converging.

The window to own the digital table is open — briefly.

⚙️

Labour crunch

Restaurants are automating service to survive staffing shortages. Self-ordering is now expected, not novel.

✨

Experience economy

Gen Z & families pay a premium for interactive, shareable, “worth-posting” dining moments.

📲

Digital dining is normal

QR menus & kiosks trained diners to tap-to-order. The tablet at the table is the natural next step.

The tabletop is the last un-digitised inch of the restaurant. We're claiming it.
